Frequently Asked Questions

How do you structure payment terms in Singapore?

add
We operate on milestone-based predictability: 50% upon project commissioning to secure studio capacity, and 50% upon final acceptance and domain DNS cutover. For our Growth Retainer, billing is processed monthly with no lock-in contract beyond a 30-day notice period.

How do you navigate Singapore MOH / HCSA medical advertising rules?

add
We are well-versed in the Ministry of Health's Healthcare Services Act (HCSA) and SMC Ethical Code guidelines. We ensure prohibited superlative claims (e.g. "Singapore's best plastic surgeon") and illicit promotional pricing hooks are avoided, structuring your site around scientific clarity, doctor credentials, and discrete patient education.

Can we migrate existing patient booking systems and WhatsApp numbers?

add
Yes. We seamlessly connect with your existing clinical management systems (such as ClinicSoft, Plato, or custom EMRs) and embed deep WhatsApp Business routing logic with customized pre-filled message parameters for specific procedures.

How long does a typical transformation take from kickoff to launch?

add
Essential packages deploy in 3 to 4 weeks. The Transform package takes 4 to 6 weeks. Signature enterprise multi-branch builds take 6 to 8 weeks. We work with strict phase deliverables so there is zero drift or prolonged delays.
