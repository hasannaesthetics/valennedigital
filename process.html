THE VALENNE METHOD

How we engineer transformation.

A disciplined, 6-step phased delivery model. We reject the uncertainty of protracted agency timelines. Every engagement is executed with rigorous phase gates, zero disruption to your daily operations, and clear architectural accountability.

01 WEEK 01 • DISCOVERY

Digital Audit & Patient Triage Diagnostic

We perform an exhaustive forensic analysis of your current website telemetry: bounce rates on high-margin procedures, mobile friction bottlenecks, and Singapore MOH / HCSA advertising exposure risks.

Deliverable: Comprehensive Digital Architecture Brief

KEY FOCUS
  • • Procedure margin mapping
  • • Mobile speed diagnostic
  • • Competitor positioning scan
02 WEEK 02 • ARCHITECTURE

Information Architecture & Conversion Flows

We restructure your sitemap and patient navigation paths. We strip away nested confusion to ensure that prospective high-net-worth clients can evaluate doctor qualifications and book discrete consultations within two taps.

Deliverable: Interactive Low-Fidelity Conversion Wireframes

KEY FOCUS
  • • High-ticket inquiry funnels
  • • Discrete WhatsApp routing flow
  • • Doctor biography credentials
03 WEEK 03 • ART DIRECTION

Editorial Visual Identity & UI Design

We craft the visual flagship layer using warm ivory tones (`#F9F7F2`), stark charcoal typography (`Playfair Display` and `Inter`), intentional whitespace, and ghost borders that exude quiet luxury.

Deliverable: High-Fidelity Responsive Prototypes (Desktop & Mobile)

KEY FOCUS
  • • Bespoke editorial layouts
  • • Curated photography art direction
  • • Tactile mobile interaction states
04 WEEK 04 • COMPLIANCE

Content Refinement & MOH / HCSA Audit

We review every headline, clinical description, and doctor accolade against the Singapore Healthcare Services Act (HCSA) advertising regulations, guaranteeing complete peace of mind before publication.

Deliverable: Final Regulatory-Approved Copy Vault

KEY FOCUS
  • • SMC ethical code compliance
  • • Scientific clinical positioning
  • • Patient privacy (PDPA) alignment
05 WEEK 05 • ENGINEERING

Sub-Second Code & Telemetry Integration

Our engineers build your website with lightweight semantic markup, achieving sub-second load speeds (< 0.8s), perfect 98+ Google Lighthouse scores, and granular event tracking on all consultation triggers.

Deliverable: Production Staging Environment with Staging Access

KEY FOCUS
  • • Zero-bloat semantic build
  • • Core Web Vitals optimization
  • • GA4 conversion telemetry
06 WEEK 06 • CUTOVER

Zero-Downtime Launch & Performance Monitoring

We execute domain DNS cutover during low-traffic clinic hours with zero operational downtime. Following launch, we monitor incoming patient triage telemetry and provide 30 to 90 days of dedicated partner warranty support.

Deliverable: Live Sovereign Flagship & Post-Launch Telemetry Report

KEY FOCUS
  • • Seamless zero-downtime DNS cutover
  • • Form deliverability verification
  • • Dedicated warranty coverage
